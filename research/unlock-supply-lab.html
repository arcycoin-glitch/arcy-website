Data rules / researched sources

On-chain supply: Arc RPC totalSupply() and ERC-20 balanceOf(). Burn proof: exact bytecode-matched reviewed contract source (Sourcify) plus Arc RPC. Unlock candidates: funded contract-linked vesting getters, GoPlus matching lock signals, or contract-selected project metadata. Tokenomist listings without an exact token-contract match are not promoted to verified values.

“No verified schedule” means not established from covered sources, not proof that the token will never unlock. Locked/unlocked totals remain unknown without complete reconciled lock coverage. This prototype currently supports Arc chain 5042 only.
