What is ARCY SEARCH?

ARCY SEARCH is a free crypto research interface covering Arc, Ethereum and Robinhood Chain. Its purpose is to help readers review token contracts and evidence for research fields such as supply and burns, holder concentration, contract controls, liquidity, token mechanics and published unlock schedules. Availability depends on each chain and independent source coverage; not all fields are verified for every token.

Third-party schedule entries are not proof that unlocks actually executed. A passing website test does not mean that any token is audited or safe to purchase.
